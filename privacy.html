Kuzco YT – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

What the extension accesses

  • YouTube (read-only): your subscriptions, and public details of videos from those channels (title, channel, upload date, duration, tags).
  • Google Drive app data folder: hidden files that hold the extension's own lists (hidden, watched and Watch Later video ids), its settings, and the titles and channel names of videos you hid, watched or liked (used for a taste analysis shown only to you). The extension cannot see or touch any other file in your Drive.

Where data is stored

Everything is stored in your own browser (extension storage) and in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. There are no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Remove the extension from your browser to delete its local data.
  • Delete the app's hidden data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.
