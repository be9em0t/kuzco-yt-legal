Kuzco YT

Kuzco YT is a personal browser extension that filters and reorders the YouTube Home feed using the channels its owner subscribes to. It is a private hobby project and is not published in any extension store.

It signs in with Google to read the owner's YouTube subscriptions (read-only) and to keep a small settings file in a hidden app folder of the owner's own Google Drive.
